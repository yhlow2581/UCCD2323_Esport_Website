Comprehensive Calculation & Deduplication Rules

To ensure fair and accurate evaluation, all point scoring must adhere to the following logic guidelines.

Principle Details
National Individual + Team Scoring At the national level, team achievements and individual MVP achievements are scored separately. Therefore, a player may receive both the team points for a National Champion/Runner-up result and an additional +2 points for receiving Tournament MVP in the same competition.
State / Campus Single-Score Rule At the state or campus level, team and individual achievements from the same competition are not stacked. Only the higher or equal applicable score is counted.
MVP Priority Rule If a Tournament MVP award is equal to or higher than the applicable team achievement for the same State / Campus competition, only the MVP points are awarded.
No Double Counting Within the Same Achievement The same specific achievement cannot be counted more than once. For example, a National Champion result gives +5 team points; it does not also receive the +2 General Participation points for the same event.
